# WWII Testimony (@WWIITestimony) - impostazioni del canale

## Descrizione del canale (da incollare in Personalizzazione > Informazioni di base > Descrizione)
When World War II ended, the questions began.

American officers sat across the table from Göring, Speer, Dönitz, Rundstedt and the men who had run Germany's war, and asked them what really happened. Hundreds of thousands of German prisoners of war crossed the Atlantic and saw America with their own eyes. Their answers were short, surprising, and rarely what you would expect.

WWII Testimony tells these stories in their own words: one question, one answer, and the history behind it. Every video is built on documented sources: Allied interrogation reports, postwar testimony, memoirs and records, checked against what actually happened. The footage is real archival film from the 1940s.

New videos every day.
